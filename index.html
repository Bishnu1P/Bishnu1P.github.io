About Me

I'm Bishnu Pradhan, a second-semester Computer Engineering student at IOE Pulchowk Campus, Lalitpur — one of Nepal's most prestigious engineering institutions. Originally from Pathari Shanischare, Morang, I came to Kathmandu with a clear goal: to become a skilled software developer who builds tools that genuinely help people.

Electronics Project

2D Defense System

A group electronics project featuring a custom-designed turret mechanism that uses a LiDAR sensor to detect objects and automatically repositions a servo-controlled gun using precise mathematical calculations. Trigonometric geometry was applied to accurately map the object's detected distance and angle to exact motor positions — merging hardware engineering with applied mathematics.

LiDAR Sensor ESP Module Servo Motor Mathematics Hardware Design
